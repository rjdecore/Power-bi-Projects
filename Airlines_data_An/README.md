# Airline Operations & Delay Analysis — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze airline operations to understand punctuality, delays, cancellations, airline performance, airport congestion, and time-based delay patterns.

## Dataset
Flight operations data from **2009–2011**, including airline, airport, flight status, date/time, and delay information.

## Reported KPIs
- **61.67% on-time flights**
- **35.9% delayed flights**
- **2.43% cancelled flights**
- **27 minutes average delay**

## Analysis Areas
- Airline-level delay performance
- On-time vs delayed vs cancelled flights
- Day and month trends
- Departure-hour patterns
- Airport-level congestion
- Best and worst performing airlines

## Business Use
The dashboard can support operational planning, scheduling, delay reduction, and service-performance monitoring.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | DAX | Time Analysis | Operational Analytics | KPI Reporting**
