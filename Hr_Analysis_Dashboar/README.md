# HR Attrition Analytics — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze workforce demographics and employee attrition to identify patterns associated with role, age, salary, education, department, gender, and tenure.

## Reported KPIs
- **1,470 employees**
- **237 attritions**
- **16.1% attrition rate**
- **37 average age**
- **7 years average tenure**

## Analysis Areas
- Attrition by education
- Attrition by age group
- Job-role attrition
- Salary-band analysis
- Gender and department comparison
- Attrition by years at company

## Business Use
The dashboard supports retention analysis, workforce planning, role-level investigation, and identification of higher-risk employee segments.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | HR Analytics | Workforce Reporting**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which employee groups contribute most to attrition?
- How does attrition vary by role, age, salary, education, gender and tenure?
- Which segments should HR investigate for retention risk?

## Key Findings to Communicate
The dashboard reports 1,470 employees, 237 attritions and a 16.1% attrition rate, with age and tenure summaries. Segment breakdowns help identify where attrition is concentrated; they do not establish why employees leave.

## Business Value
HR can prioritize follow-up interviews, manager reviews and retention initiatives for segments with higher observed attrition, then track changes over time.
