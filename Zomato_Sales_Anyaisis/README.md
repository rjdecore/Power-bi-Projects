# Zomato Restaurant Analysis — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze restaurant listings across countries, cities, cuisines, ratings, cost, online delivery, and table-booking availability to understand restaurant-market patterns.

## Dataset
Global restaurant listing data containing country, city, cuisine, ratings, average cost, online delivery, and table-booking information.

## Reported KPIs
- **9,551 restaurants**
- **1.20K average cost for two**
- **2.67 average rating**
- **146 cuisines**
- **26% online delivery availability**
- **12% table-booking availability**

## Business Questions
- Which countries and cities have the highest restaurant concentration?
- Which cuisines are most common?
- How do ratings and cost vary?
- How widespread are delivery and table-booking services?

## Business Use
The analysis can support market selection, restaurant positioning, cuisine strategy, and service-feature analysis.

## Power BI Skills
- DAX KPI measures
- Geospatial visuals
- Interactive filtering
- Multi-dimensional restaurant analysis

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | DAX | Power Query | Geospatial Analysis | Restaurant Analytics**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which countries and cities contain the most listed restaurants?
- Which cuisines are common and how do ratings and cost vary?
- How prevalent are online delivery and table booking?

## Key Findings & Business Value
The dashboard reports 9,551 restaurants, average cost for two of 1.20K, average rating 2.67, 146 cuisines, 26% online delivery availability and 12% table-booking availability. Geography and cuisine views help compare listed supply and service availability; they do not directly measure restaurant profitability.
