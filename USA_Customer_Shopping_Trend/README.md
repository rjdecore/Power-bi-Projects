# USA Customer Shopping Trends — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze customer shopping behavior in the USA across products, demographics, payment methods, subscriptions, seasons, and purchasing patterns.

## Executive KPIs
The existing dashboard reports approximately:
- **4K customers**
- **$233K sales**
- **3.75 average review rating**
- **$59.76 average order value**
- **97.9% repeat purchase rate**
- **44 average customer age**

## Analysis Areas
### Product & Customer
- Sales by category, gender, state, size, color, and individual item
- Customer and order KPIs
- Product preference analysis

### Payment & Subscription
- Payment-method contribution
- Seasonal sales
- Shipping methods
- Promo-code usage
- Subscriber vs non-subscriber performance

## Power BI Skills
- Data modeling
- DAX measures
- KPI design
- Slicers and interactive filtering
- Multi-page dashboard storytelling

## Tech Stack
**Power BI Desktop | DAX | Data Modeling | Customer Analytics | Retail Analytics**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which products, states and customer segments contribute most to shopping activity?
- How do payment, season, shipping, promotions and subscription patterns vary?
- What repeat-purchase patterns appear in the dataset?

## Key Findings & Business Value
The dashboard reports about 4K customers, $233K sales, 3.75 average review rating, $59.76 average order value, 97.9% repeat-purchase rate and average age 44. Use the segment views to describe customer differences and inform assortment, promotions and subscriber engagement analysis.
