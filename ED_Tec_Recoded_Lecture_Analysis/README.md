# EdTech Recorded Lecture Analytics — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze recorded-course offerings and learner engagement to understand category demand, language preferences, instructor performance, subtitles, skills, and course duration.

## Dataset
Approximately **20K+ course records** containing category, sub-category, language, views, ratings, duration, instructor, skills, and subtitle availability.

## Business Questions
- Which categories and sub-categories are most represented?
- Which categories generate the highest average views?
- Which skills are most frequently taught?
- What languages dominate learner demand?
- Does subtitle availability relate to engagement?
- Which instructors rank highest by rating?
- How does course duration relate to views?
- Does skill diversity relate to category viewership?

## Business Use
Insights can guide course portfolio expansion, localization, instructor partnerships, accessibility, and content design.

## Power BI Skills
- Power Query transformations
- DAX measures
- Category and instructor ranking
- Interactive dashboard design

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | EdTech Analytics | Engagement Analysis**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which course categories and skills are most represented?
- Which categories attract the most views, and which instructors have strong ratings?
- How do language, subtitles and duration relate to engagement?
- Where could the course catalogue be expanded or localized?

## Key Findings to Communicate
Use category, language, skill, instructor and view visuals to identify catalogue concentration and engagement differences. Interpret these as descriptive patterns in the recorded-course dataset, not proof that one course attribute causes higher views.

## Business Value
Learning-product teams can prioritize content gaps, localization, accessibility and instructor partnerships using the observed demand and engagement patterns.
