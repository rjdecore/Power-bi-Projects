# Call Centre Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze call-centre operations to understand call volume, resolution performance, response speed, agent performance, and customer satisfaction.

## Dataset
Call-centre logs covering **January–March 2021**, including agents, calls answered/resolved, satisfaction score, answer speed, topic, and date.

## Reported KPIs
- **618 calls answered**
- **81.23% resolution rate**
- **18.77% unresolved**
- **3.4/5 average customer satisfaction**
- **68.2 seconds average speed of answer**

## Analysis Areas
- Monthly call-volume trends
- Agent performance comparison
- Resolution and answer rates
- Customer satisfaction
- Response-speed monitoring

## Business Use
The dashboard helps identify training needs, service bottlenecks, response-time gaps, and high/low-performing agents.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | DAX | Time-Series Analysis | KPI Reporting | Customer Service Analytics**

## Business Questions Addressed
- How does call volume vary by month and topic?
- What share of calls are answered or resolved?
- How do agent metrics and customer satisfaction differ?
- Where do answer-speed bottlenecks appear?

## Key Findings & Business Value
The dashboard reports 618 calls answered, an 81.23% resolution rate, 18.77% unresolved, 3.4/5 average satisfaction and 68.2 seconds average speed of answer. Monthly, topic and agent views help identify service pressure points. Managers can use these patterns to review training, staffing and response-time improvement.
