# Pizza Sales Analytics — SQL + Power BI

## Business Objective
Combine SQL-based KPI analysis with Power BI dashboarding to understand pizza revenue, order volume, product demand, category mix, size preference, and time-based ordering patterns.

## Dataset
One year of pizza sales data covering **January–December 2015**.

## Reported KPIs
- **₹817,860 revenue**
- **21,350 orders**
- **49,574 pizzas sold**
- **₹38.31 average order value**
- **2.32 pizzas per order**

## Business Questions
- Which days and months generate the most orders?
- Which categories contribute most revenue?
- Which sizes are most popular?
- Which pizzas are the best and worst sellers?
- How do revenue and quantity vary by product?

## Workflow
**SQL exploration → KPI calculation → business analysis → Power BI visualization**

## Tech Stack
**MySQL | SQL | Power BI | DAX | Sales Analytics | Data Visualization**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which days and months generate the most orders?
- Which pizza categories, sizes and products drive revenue or quantity?
- Which products are top and bottom performers?
- What is the average order value and average pizzas per order?

## Key Findings to Communicate
The dashboard reports ₹817,860 revenue, 21,350 orders, 49,574 pizzas sold, ₹38.31 average order value and 2.32 pizzas per order. Use the product and time visuals to describe demand concentration and seasonality; avoid claiming causes that are not tested by the data.

## Business Value
Restaurant managers can plan inventory and staffing around demand patterns, review menu mix, and target promotions toward underperforming products or time slots.
