# Hospital Emergency Room Performance — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze emergency-room patient flow, admissions, wait times, referrals, demographics, workload peaks, and satisfaction to identify operational improvement opportunities.

## Dataset
**9,216 unique patients** covering **April 2023–October 2024**.

## Reported KPIs
- **50% admission rate**
- **35.3 minutes average wait**
- **4.99/10 average satisfaction**
- **59.3% attended within 30 minutes**

## Analysis Areas
- Patient demographics
- Admission vs non-admission
- Referral patterns
- Wait-time performance
- Satisfaction
- Busiest days and hours

## Business Use
The dashboard highlights staffing bottlenecks, referral demand, service-speed gaps, and patient-experience opportunities.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Healthcare Operations | KPI Reporting**
