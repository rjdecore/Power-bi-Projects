A portfolio of completed Power BI case studies focused on business reporting, sales performance, customer behavior, operations, healthcare, HR, retail and service analytics. Each project connects dashboard KPIs and visual analysis to business questions, findings and practical recommendations.

**Primary tools:** Power BI Desktop · Power Query · DAX · Data Modeling · Excel · SQL

## Business analysis workflow

1. Translate the business objective into dashboard KPIs and questions.
2. Prepare and profile data in Power Query.
3. Organize relationships and tables for clear filter behavior.
4. Use DAX measures to calculate performance and comparisons.
5. Compare segments, trends, product/customer groups and operational outcomes.
6. Interpret dashboard findings and turn them into specific business actions.

## Skills demonstrated
