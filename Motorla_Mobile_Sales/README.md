# Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze mobile-device sales across time, models, payment methods, customer demographics, cities, and customer satisfaction.

## Reported KPIs
- **769M total sales**
- **19K units sold**
- **4K transactions**
- **40K average price**
- **3.69/5 average customer satisfaction**

## Analysis Areas
- Monthly sales and quantity trends
- Top and bottom models
- Payment-method share
- Customer ratings
- Sales by day and age group
- City-level performance

## Business Use
The dashboard helps identify high-performing products, customer segments, payment preferences, and geographic opportunities.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Sales Analytics | Customer Analytics**
