# Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## Business Objective
Analyze mobile-device sales across time, models, payment methods, customer demographics, cities, and customer satisfaction.

## Reported KPIs
- **769M total sales**
- **19K units sold**
- **4K transactions**
- **40K average price**
- **3.69/5 average customer satisfaction**

## Analysis Areas
- Monthly sales and quantity trends
- Top and bottom models
- Payment-method share
- Customer ratings
- Sales by day and age group
- City-level performance

## Business Use
The dashboard helps identify high-performing products, customer segments, payment preferences, and geographic opportunities.

## Tech Stack
**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Sales Analytics | Customer Analytics**

## Business Questions Addressed
- Which mobile models, cities and customer groups contribute most to sales?
- How do sales and units sold change over time?
- Which payment methods are used most often?
- How are customer satisfaction scores distributed?

## Key Findings to Communicate
The dashboard reports 769M total sales, 19K units, 4K transactions, 40K average price and 3.69/5 average satisfaction. Use model, city, age and payment breakdowns to explain where performance is concentrated; verify the selected filters before quoting any segment result.

## Business Value
Retail and product teams can prioritize inventory review, city-level campaigns, model assortment and customer-experience follow-up.
